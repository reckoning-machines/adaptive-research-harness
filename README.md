# New Speedway

An operating system for an evolving quantitative investment process:
agents generate research, write strategies and evolve their own
research methods, while every consequential change to the investment
process is authorized, budgeted, independently checked and recorded as
governed state. Strategies are disposable; the evidence and the
governed process are the assets.

**Status (2026-10-19):** pre-implementation design, complete in
documents, awaiting the final independent whole-design review. Every
admission is a document-phase admission by the Principal (the human
owner). No code, runtime selection, executed genesis, CPOC witness or
certification exists. Nothing here claims an edge.
