# Research on the Principal's Own Documents - Direction and Extension

Status: **PRINCIPAL DIRECTION RECORDED. DISPOSABLE DEVELOPMENT UNDER THE ADDENDUM'S WORKFLOW PARAGRAPH. CV1 UNCHANGED. CONSTITUTIONAL DELTA 0/0/0.**

Date: 2026-11-01. The [integration plan](../plans/langgraph_integration_20261101.md),
section 9, said that research on real documents needs a small core
extension to the meaning of document and research records, and that this
goes to the Principal as one decision before it is built. This record holds
that direction and the scope of the extension.

## Direction, verbatim

> The next milestone is me using ARH for an actual research question.
>
> Identify and implement the smallest extension that lets me bring my own
> documents, preserve their provenance, record an expectation, run and check
> an analysis, and use the result to choose the next investigation. Preserve
> revisions and rejected conclusions, and let me stop and resume through the
> workbench.
>
> Make the next-action choice respond to the research evidence rather than a
> fixed demonstration script. Distinguish what the researcher proposes from
> what the harness verifies and permits.
>
> Start with local documents and calculations. Identify separately any
> provider access and spending needed for an LLM researcher. Keep this
> focused on one usable research workflow, with no strategy allocation,
> paper execution or general platform expansion.

## The extension

A document class. A source rule declares its documents `synthetic` or
`historical`. A historical document is real material read after the fact.
It keeps its file name, content hash, size, media type and declared
provenance, and the ledger stamps its class. A research record that cites
a historical document is itself historical. Neither class earns forward
standing, which CV1 reserves for forward predictions that are not built.
Nothing is labelled synthetic to pass validation.

Two shapes the existing rules did not allow and now do, under the same
controls. A research record of kind `EXPERIMENT DESIGN` or `SELECTION` may
be committed without a metered attempt, so an expectation can be recorded
before the analysis that tests it. An attempt may cite several documents
and one design record as its exact inputs, so the analysis the worker runs
is pinned to the committed expectation. A source may hold many items per
period, identified by the document content hash.

Nothing else changes in authority or meaning. Reservation, attempt,
settlement, independent check, decision and Apply are unchanged. The next
investigation is proposed as a Theme by the researcher, checked by a
separate evaluator, and admitted or rejected by the Principal.

## Not in scope

Real providers, real spending, strategy allocation, paper execution and
any general platform expansion. What an LLM researcher would need is
identified in the implementation report, not built.
