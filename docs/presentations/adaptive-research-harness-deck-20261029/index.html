Introduce the constitutional research harness and its proposed scope. Then the premise. Every edge is temporary, and so are the research methods that find them. The question worth money is whether the process that finds edges can get better over time. The cards show strategies coming and going; the band underneath is the thing we keep. Nothing in this deck claims an edge was found.

Today’s failed backtest should improve tomorrow’s question.

Adaptive Research Harness keeps what was tried, what happened and what to investigate next. One research journey:

The trade failed. The research got better. The failed trade becomes evidence for the next decision.

Synthetic illustration, not a governed execution. The hypothesis, the evidence and the rejection all stay on the record.

This is the slide that carries the deck. Walk the four steps slowly. The point is the last card: a failed trade did not end the line of research, it sharpened it, and the next test has a stated reason: the synthetic pattern is uneconomic at the tested size. Time and budget were spent; what they bought is evidence. In the design, the hypothesis is committed before the window, the outcome is computed from declared sources, the losing strategy is reduced or retired by a decision that is independently checked, and the next experiment is chosen from the recorded evidence. The narrower follow-up hypothesis reuses the earlier research but must earn its own record.

A profitable strategy can have the wrong explanation.

So the explanation, the trade and the research method are judged separately, each on its own record.

The hypothesis

Was the explanation supported?

Judged by its own predictions, committed before the outcome was knowable.

The strategy

Did the trade make money after costs?

Judged on its own forward record, net of modelled costs, within risk limits.

The research method

Did the researcher produce useful work?

Judged on research quality for the budget, never on a count of appealing backtests.

An agent proposes a better research method. We compare it with its predecessor on new tasks, including the cost.

A right explanation can lose money. A winning trade can rest on a wrong one. Adaptive Research Harness records which, and acts on each.

No score in one competition feeds another unless a recorded decision says so.

Three populations, three questions. In the design, hypotheses compete for belief, strategies compete for paper capital, and research agents compete on contribution per unit of budget. The separation is a rule, not a habit: a strategy citing a hypothesis transfers no standing to it, and a hypothesis gains standing only from its own committed predictions.

Use existing tools. Build the research process.

The core tools already exist. The missing part is the system that directs them and learns from the results.

We will use existing resources for

  • Open-source backtesting
  • Existing AI models
  • Market data and document sources
  • Established workflow and storage software
  • Internal paper-fill computation (later)

We will build Adaptive Research Harness to

  • Turn investment questions into defined experiments
  • Propose and challenge ideas
  • Preserve evidence, including failed tests
  • Coordinate research within budgets
  • Evaluate strategies and propose what happens next

We are not building another backtesting engine or training a foundation model. We are building the system that directs the research and learns from its results.

Specific products remain under evaluation. The reuse strategy does not depend on which ones are chosen.

Each row pairs a thing we rent or adopt with a thing we build. The rule behind the pairing: a tool may compute, store, fetch or run, but it never owns a governed fact. Models, data feeds, backtesters, workflow engines and internal fill computation are replaceable without changing what Adaptive Research Harness knows. No runtime, model provider or data vendor has been selected, and the design does not require any particular one.

The research process itself is an object of research.

No runtime has been selected. Candidates will be judged on one concrete sequence: budget, interruption, revocation, retry and admission.

The point of this slide: the research process itself is an object of research. Say it in one breath: we will use an existing agent runtime where it fits. What I am designing sits above it: a population of research methods that proposes investment ideas, tests them, retains the evidence and proposes improvements to the research process itself. The runtime executes the work. Adaptive Research Harness determines what the results mean and what changes are permitted. The runtime never owns a governed fact: its saved progress is a cache, and Adaptive Research Harness reconstructs the lawful next step from its own record if the runtime is replaced.

More autonomous research. Explicit human control.

Agents can investigate and propose. Budget and capital decisions remain governed.

Agents may, within a mandate

  • Investigate changes in the world and patterns in data
  • Propose hypotheses and strategies
  • Run backtests and paper tests inside a budget
  • Propose improved research methods and test them against earlier versions
  • Register routine hypotheses after an independent check

Only you decide

  • Every grant of budget and scope
  • Every allocation of paper capital, up or down
  • The standard everything is judged by
  • Stopping new spending, at any moment
  • Any change to the rules themselves

Every change is independently checked and recorded. You can see why something changed, stop new spending, and recover without losing the record.

An agent holds no right it was not granted. A new agent version inherits research, never permissions.

Translate the machinery into outcomes here, not mechanisms. Behind this slide: every consequential change is a Transition that is proposed, independently checked, decided and applied in order. Research budgets are reserved before work and settled after. A funding stop takes effect immediately for new spending, while admitted work still settles. If a process dies mid-flight, a fresh one reconstructs the lawful next step from the record, never from memory.
