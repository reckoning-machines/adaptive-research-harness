ADAPTIVE RESEARCH HARNESS

Two boundaries for the first research cycle

Transition ledger

Propose → independent Check → decide → Apply

Applied History owns membership, standing, grants and current versions.

Only Apply changes the population. A stale base refuses.

Evidence ledger

Reserve → attempt → result or failure → settle

Owns research records, funding stops and binding records. Supplies the clock.

Pending liabilities and negative results survive interruption.

Principal grants and decides. Researcher proposes. Checker verifies independently.
Tools compute; they cannot write either ledger or admit their own results.

The full CV1 design also defines a separate Paper Book. It is excluded from the first milestone; future simulation needs its own authorized expansion.

Design only. CV1 governs; no core, genesis or conformance witness has run.
