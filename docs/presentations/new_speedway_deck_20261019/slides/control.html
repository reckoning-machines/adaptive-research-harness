More autonomous research. Explicit human control.

Agents can investigate and propose. Budget and capital decisions remain governed.

Agents may, within a mandate

  • Investigate changes in the world and patterns in data
  • Propose hypotheses and strategies
  • Run backtests and paper tests inside a budget
  • Propose improved research methods and test them against earlier versions
  • Register routine hypotheses after an independent check

Only you decide

  • Every grant of budget and scope
  • Every allocation of paper capital, up or down
  • The standard everything is judged by
  • Stopping new spending, at any moment
  • Any change to the rules themselves

Every change is independently checked and recorded. You can see why something changed, stop new spending, and recover without losing the record.

An agent holds no right it was not granted. A new agent version inherits research, never permissions.

Translate the machinery into outcomes here, not mechanisms. Behind this slide: every consequential change is a Transition that is proposed, independently checked, decided and applied in order. Research budgets are reserved before work and settled after. A funding stop takes effect immediately for new spending, while admitted work still settles. If a process dies mid-flight, a fresh one reconstructs the lawful next step from the record, never from memory.
