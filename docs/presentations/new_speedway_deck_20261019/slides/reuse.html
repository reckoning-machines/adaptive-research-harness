Use existing tools. Build the research process.

The core tools already exist. The missing part is the system that directs them and learns from the results.

We will use existing resources for

  • Open-source backtesting
  • Existing AI models
  • Market data and document sources
  • Established workflow and storage software
  • Suitable paper-trading tools

We will build Speedway to

  • Turn investment questions into defined experiments
  • Propose and challenge ideas
  • Preserve evidence, including failed tests
  • Coordinate research within budgets
  • Evaluate strategies and propose what happens next

We are not building another backtesting engine or training a foundation model. We are building the system that directs the research and learns from its results.

Specific products remain under evaluation. The reuse strategy does not depend on which ones are chosen.

Each row pairs a thing we rent or adopt with a thing we build. The rule behind the pairing: a tool may compute, store, fetch or run, but it never owns a governed fact. Models, data feeds, backtesters, workflow engines and paper-trading venues are replaceable without changing what Speedway knows. No runtime, model provider or data vendor has been selected, and the design does not require any particular one.
