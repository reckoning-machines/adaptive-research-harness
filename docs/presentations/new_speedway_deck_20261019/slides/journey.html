This is the slide that carries the deck. Walk the four steps slowly. The point is the last card: a failed trade did not end the line of research, it sharpened it, and the next test has a stated reason: the effect is real but uneconomic at the observed size. Time and budget were spent; what they bought is evidence. In the design, the hypothesis is committed before the window, the outcome is computed from declared sources, the losing strategy is reduced or retired by a decision that is independently checked, and the next experiment is chosen from the recorded evidence. The narrower follow-up hypothesis reuses the earlier research but must earn its own record.
