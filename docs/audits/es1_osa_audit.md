# OSA Audit of ES-1 as Proposed (PD-005, revision 3)

Status: **AUDIT — EVIDENCE, NOT DOCTRINE. Authorizes nothing. Certifies
nothing.**

As-of: 2026-10-19. Subject:
[PD-005, proposed, revision 3](../decisions/principal_decision_005_es1_evaluation_standard.md),
the initial Evaluation Standard, after the Principal's five repair
directions (§6) and the cost clarification (§7). Reconciliation standard: Constitution
revision 5 (§III.5 Evaluation record, §III.9, I-1, I-3, I-4, I-7, I-13,
Part V); fin123-core forward evidentiary doctrine §5, §6, §8, §9, §10,
§11; ANF and the seven authority invariants. Scope: whether ES-1's
requirements are expressible as Evaluation content under existing
authority, whether any method or score acquires a decision right,
whether the three cases and ten challenge cases are answered from the
record alone, and whether the carried items are resolved without
reopening admitted decisions.

## Executive disposition

**A− — COHERENT. `ΔO = 0`, `ΔA = 0`, `ΔS` confined to Evaluation
content, Standard content, registration content, one widened Research
Record meaning and derived views. No contradiction with revision 5 and
no remaining contradiction after the five repairs. Ready for the
Principal's decision as a Constitutional Transition, with every constant
provisional.**

## 1. Authority check

| Question | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Who computes an Evaluation? | Any authorized agent or human under its Authorization (Part V, other Evidence commit), unchanged. The proposer may compute its own; the Checker recomputes at Check (R-10) |
| Does any output change standing or capital? | NO. §5 of the record: outcomes inform Transition classes; Apply alone changes standing or allocation (§III.6, I-13) |
| Does a method acquire authority? | NO. Methods are versioned artifacts committed before the window they judge and pinned in Evaluations; replacement is a successor commitment before the next window. A register of permitted methods was considered and rejected as a new authority over methods; the Principal may still restrict methods through Checker Authorization content if wanted |
| Who owns the method commitment, and which boundary writes it? | The initial commitment is registration content, owned by the Transition ledger as proposal content and fixed at the registration's Apply; a successor commitment is a Research Record of kind `EXPERIMENT DESIGN`, owned by the Evidence ledger and written at Evidence commit by an agent under its existing Authorization. Both carriers exist; the only change is the kind's widened meaning (`ΔS`). No gap, no workaround |
| Is cost verification a new Checker right? | NO. It is Check content: sequence of the cost commitment, comparison with market evidence (spreads, volume, depth, fees) read from declared Market Observations or recorded in the Check with provenance, the distinction from modelled impact and simulated fills, and refusal when cost uncertainty could eliminate the edge. The Checker still cannot admit, no cost engine is designed, and no new source authority is created: evidence cited in a Check is either an Observation under an Observation Authorization or a recorded Check observation with provenance |
| Does `a*` or an accompanying `Investigate` create a second standing? | NO. One primary outcome per Evaluation; `a*` is a derived number the Allocation Transition may cite; accompanying questions are content and inform Research Authorization acts only |
| Does ES-1 give the Checker a new right? | NO. The Check content is extended (conformance of the cited Evaluation); the Checker still cannot admit |
| Does any population's score enter another's? | NO. §4 keeps three output sets; I-1 forbids transfer; a lineage is credited with forward-resolved research, not with profit; a Strategy's record is not raised by its Hypothesis |
| Can an Evaluation under a later `ES` version restate an earlier Transition's basis? | NO. Pinning (§III.6): each applied Transition carries its `EvaluationStandardVersion`; R-10 reports cross-version comparison as a standard change |
| Is any constant fitted to the record it judges? | Each constant in §6 declares its basis (doctrine §11); the "three times" discontinuity is an unfitted stress; the minimums are not derived from any forward record because none exists yet |
| Does ES-1 reopen an admitted decision? | NO. FR-6 is resolved by a derived view over existing standings, leaving the PD-001 boundary immutable; FR-8 restates I-2 and I-3 for grounding; PD-004's non-conformance rule is applied, not changed |
| Stop conditions | No new identity, owner, boundary, kind or authority; every new meaning is Standard content decided by the Principal through a Constitutional Transition |

## 8. Admission recommendation

PD-005 revision 2 is expressible entirely as Evaluation content,
Standard content, registration content and derived views under existing
authority. It makes no method
constitutional, lets no score decide, keeps the three competitions
apart, grounds only on the forward class, counts the family, sizes by
survivability, and names what it cannot estimate. Recommend the
Principal propose it as a Constitutional Transition admitting `ES-1.0`,
with every constant in §6 labelled provisional, after deciding the eight
questions in §10 of the record.

## 9. What this audit does not establish

That any method will estimate `N_eff` well, that the stress set will
contain the event that matters, that twenty effective events are enough,
or that an edge found under ES-1 is real. It establishes that ES-1 can
be applied from the ledgers alone, verified by a Checker, and changed
only by the Principal.
