## 9. Admission recommendation

PD-007 is coherent against revision 5, declares its one authority
change explicitly and revocably, makes independence a matter of
authority, protected standards and immutable evidence rather than of
providers, gives the Check one record form, closes U-10 without a new
function, and prevents concurrent overspend without a queue. Recommend
the Principal decide it after PD-005, since its Check content cites
`ES-1`'s requirements.

## 10. What this audit does not establish

That any Checker will catch what matters, that `P-1`'s conditions are
the right ones for a second policy, or that ten agents will produce
research worth checking. It establishes that no agent can admit, no
Checker can decide or rewrite criteria, no policy can interpret, and no
two agents can spend the same unit twice.
