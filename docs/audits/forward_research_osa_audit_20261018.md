## Executive disposition

**C+ — THE TESTING HALF FITS; THE FORMATION HALF IS UNGOVERNED. Revision 2
specifies the formation half without adding an object.**

From the moment a Hypothesis exists, the state model supports forward
research well: committed Predictions over bound resolution sets, Outcomes,
Strategy Decisions over closures, incubation at zero allocation, and
separate competitions for belief, capital and research contribution. Both
worked paths run under the same rules from that point on.

Before a Hypothesis exists, the model was silent or wrong in five places
(FR-1, FR-3, FR-5, FR-6, FR-7). Revision 2 adds four findings from the
Principal's questions:

- **Discovery (FR-10).** Discovery is governed by declared feed sources
  under the Observation Authorization, a discovery scope on the Research
  Authorization, screening records that preserve every rejected candidate,
  and a one-hop provenance rule for follow-up retrieval. `SRC-ADHOC` is
  withdrawn; no Authorization grants open information access.
- **Document resolution (FR-11).** The categorical ban on document
  Observations resolving Predictions is withdrawn. The constitutional line
  is formation versus later independent arrival. A documentary disclosure
  may resolve a Prediction when source, field meaning, time and extraction
  rule were declared at commitment and the extraction is performed by the
  Observation function or a Checker, never by the proposer.
- **Serial admission (FR-13).** Revision 1's example applied paired
  Transitions against one base, violating I-9, and merged a Theme
  registration with an Authorization grant, for which no combined class
  exists. The example is re-sequenced: every Transition names its base,
  stale bases are refused at Apply and re-checked, and the grant is its own
  Transition.
- **Exploratory paper operation (FR-12).** Three capital standings are
  distinguished: `INCUBATING` (zero allocation), `EXPLORATORY` (bounded
  paper allocation from a Principal-declared pool, for execution
  measurement, labelled, never evidence of alpha), and `QUALIFIED`
  (evidence-qualified allocation on the forward record under the
  Standard). No numerical threshold is set and `ES-1` is not changed.

Total proposed delta across all repairs: `ΔO = 0`; `ΔS` non-zero in
§III.1, §III.2, §III.3, §III.5, §III.6, §III.7, Part V and I-5's reading;
`ΔA` non-zero only as scopes within the existing Authorization object and
one commit restriction on extraction records. No admission authority
moves. No agent gains a commit right.

Research selection has a clear authority (the Principal, or an admitted
policy under D-11) for the decision to fund a Theme, and no authority at
all for the method that proposes what to fund. The audit recommends that
the method be an Agent Design lineage, which makes it measurable and
replaceable under rules that already exist.

## 1. The objective, restated as test conditions

The system must be able to: notice a change in markets from information it
was not told to look at; decide, under a budget, whether the change is
worth a question; pose competing questions as falsifiable claims; commit
answers before reality resolves them; turn a claim into an executable
Strategy that trades prices and flows; incubate it without capital; fund it
on its own forward record; and retire the claim, the Strategy or the method
that found them when they fail, keeping what was learned. Every step must
be a governed act with an owner, or a derived view with none.

Prices and flows govern trading. The system is not a fundamental research
platform. Historical backtesting is one path, not the path.

## 2. Audit of the Theme → Hypothesis → Strategy path

Each question is answered against the exact text and given a verdict:
SUPPORTED, SILENT (missing semantics), or CONTRADICTION.

### 2.1 How does an agent discover a potential Theme?

Text: §III.1 "A Research Agent may propose a Theme; the proposal is a
Transition." §III.7: an Authorization names "the Themes it may work in"
and a research budget. §II: research budget is "allocated through
Authorizations to Themes and Agent Designs."

Finding: every unit of research effort is attributed to a Theme through
the Authorization. Work that precedes any Theme, which is exactly the work
of discovering one, has no Authorization under which to run and no
Research Record under which to be recorded. **CONTRADICTION** between the
thesis (Part I.3) and the authority map. Defect **FR-1**.

Revision 1 proposed an "exploration scope" (budget units not tied to a
Theme). The Principal's question showed that budget alone does not make
discovery autonomous: an agent still needs a lawful way to *encounter*
information it was not pointed at. Revision 2 specifies the encounter,
selection, rejection and bounds in §3.1, as defect **FR-10**, and folds
the exploration scope into a **discovery scope**.

### 2.2 What information may it investigate before the Theme exists?

Text: Evidence has five kinds (§III.5). Market Observation is a `RECEIPT`
from a declared numerical source. Research Record names "the question, the
result". Nothing names a document, a filing, a locator, a content hash or
a reading.

Finding: there is no governed carrier for "the agent read this document at
this time". **SILENT**. Defect **FR-5**, treated in §3.2.

### 2.3 How does it determine that a Theme merits research resources?

Text: §III.1 "Theme standing is a research-direction decision, never an
evaluation result." Part V: Theme registration is proposed by a Research
Agent or the Principal, checked, decided by the Principal.

Finding: authority is clear. What is missing is any required content
stating *why*. **SILENT**. Defect **FR-7**, treated in §5.

### 2.4 How does it construct competing Hypotheses from a Theme?

Finding: two Hypotheses compete when they commit Predictions over the same
resolution set with incompatible targets. Expressible today without a new
relation. An experiment that distinguishes them is a Research Record of
kind `EXPERIMENT DESIGN` that names the resolution set and must precede
the Predictions it names. **SUPPORTED** with that kind added.

### 2.5 Can one Hypothesis emerge from multiple Themes?

Text: §III.1 "A Hypothesis belongs to at most one Theme." Discussion paper
§4: `motivated_by` is many-to-many.

Finding: **CONTRADICTION** with the explanatory-graph intent. Defect
**FR-3**. Repair: `motivated_by` is a set of Themes and Research Records
in the registration proposal; funding is exactly one Authorization.
Lineage memory (I-5) remains a `DERIVED` view and handles sets.

### 2.6 How are predictions committed before their outcomes become known?

Finding: **SUPPORTED** by PD-001 and PD-002. For a target whose resolving
source has no absence-witness slot or structural bound, no forward record
is obtainable; the Checker's registration refusal makes this visible at
registration rather than at scoring.

### 2.7 How do Strategies consume forward Predictions without inheriting their standing?

Finding: **SUPPORTED** by PD-002.1 closure rule 4 and I-1.

### 2.8 How does the system respond when the Theme remains interesting but its Hypotheses fail?

Finding: **SUPPORTED** in authority. One gap, **FR-4** (minor): nothing
stops a lineage re-asking a closed question under a new id; the Checker
should read the Theme's lineage memory and refuse a registration that
restates a contradicted Hypothesis without a declared difference.

### 2.9 How does a research agent revise its research approach or create a successor agent?

Finding: **SUPPORTED** by §III.4, T-C, I-5, I-6. D-14 (provider in
identity) and succession rate remain open and are not forward-research
specific.

### Path verdict

Formation (2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.5): two contradictions, two silences, now
specified in §3. Testing and succession: supported.

## 3. Discovery and unstructured research provenance

### 3.1 Autonomous Theme discovery (FR-10)

The requirement: the system must find candidate Themes in new filings,
announcements, product launches, positioning data and market-structure
changes, without an agent already holding the URL, while staying within
declared source access and research budgets, and while preserving what it
rejected.

**(a) Encounter: declared feed sources, retrieved by the Observation
function.**

The Observation Authorization (PD-002.2) declares **feed sources**
alongside numerical sources. A feed source is a `SourceId` whose
retrieval result is a list of new items since the last slot, each
committed as a `DOCUMENT`-form Observation: locator, content hash,
claimed publication time, witnessed receipt time, the feed `SourceId` and
revision, and the slot. Examples of feed sources, each a Principal act to
declare: a regulator's daily new-filing index; an exchange's new-listing
and rule-filing notices; an issuer's product-launch page; a weekly
positioning report; a central bank's release calendar. The agent does not
fetch; the ledger fills on schedule under an Authorization no agent holds.
This is the same independence PD-002.2 established for prices, applied to
documents.

**(b) Selection: screening records.**

A Research Authorization may carry a **discovery scope**: the feed
`SourceId`s the agent may screen; a unit budget for screening and
follow-up not attributed to any Theme (revision 1's exploration units);
a cap on follow-up retrievals per period; and the hop rule in (c). Under
it the agent commits Research Records of kind `SCREEN`: for each feed
item examined, its Observation sequence, typed claims (§3.2 (c)), and a
disposition `CANDIDATE`, `DEFERRED` or `REJECTED` with a stated reason.
Screening consumes declared units per record. A `SCREEN` record is a
Research Record like any other: immutable, attributed to the Agent Design
Version and Authorization, with a closure.

**(c) Follow-up: the one-hop provenance rule, replacing `SRC-ADHOC`.**

Revision 1 allowed on-request retrieval of any locator under `SRC-ADHOC`.
The Principal's concern is right: that is unrestricted information access
by another name. Withdrawn. In its place:

| Rule | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Hop | An on-request retrieval must cite the `DOCUMENT` Observation in which the requested locator appears (the referencing item), and the cited item must be a feed item under the requester's discovery scope, or an item one hop from one. The Observation function verifies the locator is present in the cited bytes before fetching |
| Depth and count | The discovery scope sets the maximum hop depth (proposed default: one) and the number of follow-ups per period |
| Domain | The Observation Authorization may list domains or sources to which hops are permitted or refused, independent of scope |
| Refusal | A request outside scope, beyond depth or count, or whose locator is not found in the cited bytes is refused by the Observation function, and the Evidence commit boundary preserves the refusal with the request |
| Growth | A locator the agent wants but cannot reach lawfully is a reason to propose a successor Observation Authorization naming a new source. That proposal is a Transition decided by the Principal. There is no other route |

`SourceId` for a hop retrieval is the feed's `SourceId` with a hop
marker, so provenance of every document traces to a declared feed and the
chain of items that led to it.

**(d) Rejected candidates are preserved.**

`REJECTED` screens are Research Records with reasons. A `DERIVED` view
over `SCREEN` records keyed by item content hash shows what every
lineage has screened and why; a later agent that re-screens the same item
sees the prior disposition (I-5 applied to screening). Nothing stops a
successor from reaching a different disposition; it must state the
difference. Rejected candidates are negative evidence about selection,
not about markets, and are counted when the selection method is judged
(§5).

**(e) Budgets and bounds.**

| Bound | Carrier |
| --- | --- |
| Which feeds an agent may screen | Discovery scope of its Research Authorization (Principal) |
| Which feeds exist and how often they are polled | Observation Authorization (Principal) |
| Screening and follow-up units | Discovery scope units; consumption by `SCREEN` and follow-up Research Records |
| Follow-up count and depth | Discovery scope caps; enforced by the Observation function at request |
| Theme-attributed research | Separate Research Authorization per Theme, as now |

Nothing here gives an agent a right it can expand. The agent proposes
sources; the Principal declares them; the Observation function retrieves
them; the agent screens what the ledger holds.

**(f) What this does not do.** It does not read the open web. A
discovery system limited to declared feeds will miss what no declared
feed carries. That is a bound the Principal sets by what feeds are
declared, visible as a list, and widened by a Transition. The alternative
(open retrieval under an agent's own judgment) was rejected because it
makes the agent the sole witness of what it saw and makes source access
unbounded.

### 3.2 Provenance of what an agent read (FR-5)

**(a) Document Observation: a form of Market Observation, not a new kind.**

Fields: `SourceId` (feed, declared document source, or feed-with-hop),
locator, content hash of the exact bytes, claimed publication time
(`RECEIPT` content), witnessed receipt time and sequence (Evidence
clock), requesting Authorization for hop retrievals, stored bytes
addressed by hash. A later retrieval of the same locator with a different
hash is a new Observation. The availability interval of a feed item has a
lower bound from the previous slot at which it was absent from the feed,
and an upper bound at first receipt; a hop retrieval has only the upper
bound.

**(b) What the agent read is its Research Record's closure.**

A Research Record records the Evidence sequences of the Observations and
Research Records it read, and the `StateVersion` it read. A claim that
cites bytes outside the closure is visibly unsupported. The agent reads
from the ledger copy; anything read elsewhere is `EPHEMERAL` and cannot be
cited.

**(c) Typed claims.**

| Type | Meaning | Must cite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `REPORTED` | A statement the document makes | A Document Observation and a location in it |
| `INTERPRETATION` | The agent's reading of reported statements | The `REPORTED` claims it rests on |
| `ASSUMPTION` | A premise adopted without a source | Nothing; declared as such |
| `QUESTION` | What the record proposes to find out | The claims that motivate it |
| `PREDICTION_REF` | A reference to a committed Prediction | The Prediction's sequence |

**(d) Formation versus resolution (FR-11, replacing revision 1's ban).**

Revision 1 said no Document Observation may resolve a Prediction. The
Principal asked for the real distinction, which is not the form of the
evidence but its relation to the claim: evidence that **formed** a claim
can never test it; evidence that **arrives later and independently** can.
Revised rule:

1. A Hypothesis registration declares a **motivation closure** (the
   Research Records and Observations that formed it). No member of the
   motivation closure, and no Observation with sequence below the
   Prediction's commitment, may be a member of a resolution set for any
   Prediction of that Hypothesis. This is the constitutional line.
2. A resolution set member may be a **document-sourced field** when the
   Prediction's resolution rule, fixed at commitment, declares: the
   `SourceId` (a declared document source or feed under an active
   Observation Authorization at commitment; never a hop); the period or
   event the document must report; the **field meaning** (what quantity,
   in what unit, as the source defines it); the print rule (first
   posting or named amendment); and the **extraction rule**: a
   deterministic rule carried in the Source Declaration (a tagged data
   element, a named table cell, a fixed parser) or, where none exists, a
   declaration that extraction is by Checker.
3. **Who extracts.** Where the Source Declaration carries a deterministic
   extraction rule, the Observation function applies it at retrieval and
   commits a `VALUE` result bound to the `DOCUMENT` Observation; the value
   is then an ordinary Market Observation and resolves like any other.
   Where extraction is by Checker, a Checker under a Checker Authorization
   commits an **extraction record** (Evaluation-kind Evidence, like a
   reproduction record) naming the Document Observation, the field
   meaning, the extracted value and its location in the bytes; the Outcome
   binds the Document Observation and the extraction record. Two
   extraction records that disagree make the Outcome `DISPUTED`, decided
   by the Principal. **The proposer may commit neither.** The proposer's
   own reading of the document is a Research Record claim and has no
   standing in resolution.
4. **Time.** The Document Observation's witnessed receipt must be after
   commitment, and the feed's prior-slot absence (or the source's
   structural bound, where the Checker accepted one at registration, for
   example a periodic report that cannot exist before its period ends)
   gives the lower bound. Where no lower bound exists, prospective
   classification is refused as for any value (PD-002).
5. **Field meaning is part of source identity.** A change in how the
   source defines the field is a new `SourceId`, under PD-002's rule that
   meaning fields are immutable. An extraction rule change is a new
   `SourceRevision`; Outcomes pin the revision used.

A document that a proposer read, summarized and cited in forming a claim
cannot resolve that claim. A document the system received later, from a
source declared before commitment, with a value extracted by someone
other than the proposer under a rule fixed before commitment, can. That
is the same rule PD-002 applies to prices, extended to fields in
documents.

**(e) Contradiction later is a new record; (f) independence of bytes from
the agent's assertion; (g) training data**: as in revision 1. The bytes
are what the Observation function fetched; the summary is the agent's
claim; formation evidence never counts; PD-002.6 applies.

### 3.3 Delta of §3

```text
ΔO: NONE. Document Observation is a result form of the Market Observation
    kind; SCREEN, EXPERIMENT DESIGN and SELECTION are Research Record
    kinds; extraction records are Evaluation-kind Evidence.
ΔS: Market Observation gains the DOCUMENT form, feed items and hop
    provenance; SRC-ADHOC withdrawn. Research Record gains a closure,
    typed claims and the kinds above. Hypothesis registration gains a
    motivation closure; resolution-set members may be document-sourced
    fields with declared field meaning and extraction rule; the
    formation/resolution line is by closure and sequence, not by form.
    Source Declaration gains optional extraction rules; field meaning is
    a meaning field of SourceId.
ΔA: Observation Authorization gains feed sources and the hop rule
    (depth, count, domains). Research Authorization gains a discovery
    scope (feeds, units, follow-up caps). Extraction records may be
    committed only under a Checker Authorization. The Observation
    function remains the only committer of Observations. No agent gains
    a commit right; no admission authority moves.
```

### 3.4 Alternatives rejected and preserved

- Agent commits Document Observations itself: rejected (second committer;
  agent witnesses its own reading).
- A "Source Document" object: rejected (no unowned fact).
- Agent's summary as a Market Observation: rejected (proposer assertion
  would become citable for resolution).
- `SRC-ADHOC` open retrieval: rejected in revision 2 (unbounded access;
  no provenance chain).
- Categorical ban on document resolution: rejected in revision 2 (wrong
  line; the right line is formation versus later independent arrival).
- Proposer-performed extraction with Checker review: rejected (the
  proposer would commit the resolving value; review after the fact is
  not independence).

### 4.4 Exploratory paper operation (FR-12)

The Constitution has one allocation notion. The example needs three
standings of a Strategy Version's capital, because measuring execution
behavior (the discussion paper's §9) requires paper orders before any
forward record exists, and that activity must never read as alpha.

| Standing | Allocation | How entered | What it produces | What it may ground |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `INCUBATING` | Zero | Strategy Version registration (membership) | Decisions; decision-implied record at the Standard's notional; reproduction records | Qualification, once `ES-1`'s forward-event minimum is met |
| `EXPLORATORY` | Bounded: from a Principal-declared **exploratory pool** within the capital and risk limits, with a per-version cap and an expiry | An Allocation Transition of purpose `EXPLORATORY`: states the measurement purpose and expiry; checked for pool capacity and composition; cites no performance Evaluation and needs none; decided by the Principal or policy | Executed record **labelled `EXPLORATORY`**; fills, turnover, timing, order clustering | Implementation-shortfall and activity measurement only. **Never alpha, never qualification.** Reports carry the label; an Allocation Transition to `QUALIFIED` may cite it for shortfall and may not cite it as the grounding series |
| `QUALIFIED` | Evidence-qualified | An Allocation Transition of purpose `QUALIFIED` citing an Evaluation under the Standard in force at base whose grounding series is the forward decision-implied record (and, once qualified, the qualified executed record) | Executed record, qualified | Further allocation, within limits |

Rules:

- Expiry of an `EXPLORATORY` allocation is a scheduled Transition (as 9b)
  that returns the version to `INCUBATING`; it is not automatic in the
  projection, because only Apply changes allocation.
- The exploratory pool is a limit in the Applied History, declared by the
  Principal like every other limit; its size is not set here.
- An `EXPLORATORY` version counts against gross and concentration limits
  like any other allocation (I-10, composition).
- No ES-1 threshold is set by this audit. `ES-1` later states the
  forward-event minimum for qualification and the notional for the
  decision-implied record. The three standings and the label are
  constitutional (§III.3, §III.6, Part V); the numbers are Standard
  content.

Delta: `ΔO = 0`; `ΔS`: capital standing values on a Strategy Version;
Allocation Transition gains a purpose and, for `EXPLORATORY`, an expiry
and measurement statement; limits gain an exploratory pool; `ΔA = 0`
(same proposers, Checker, deciders).

## 5. Value of Information and research selection

### 5.1 Does Applied Information Economics fit?

| AIE element | New Speedway object or fact | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The decision | A Transition class: fund a Theme, register a Hypothesis, allocate capital, retire | Principal or admitted policy |
| The uncertain variables | Hypothesis truth values; effect sizes; costs; adoption | Nobody; estimates are Research Record content |
| The measurement | An experiment design and the Predictions it commits | Research Record; Evidence |
| Value of information | Expected change in a decision from the measurement, at a declared horizon | A declared estimate in the selection basis (FR-7); never authoritative |
| Cost | Research units and calendar time to resolution | Research Record consumption; Authorization grant |
| Stopping | A declared stopping rule; the Theme's standing Transition when reached | Principal or policy |

Good fit for the decision structure; poor for the arithmetic, since AIE
assumes a fixed decision and payoff distribution while information is
gathered. The estimate must carry its horizon and be compared with what
happened, which requires that it was recorded.

### 5.2 Where AIE sits

A selection method is an **Agent Design lineage** whose outputs are
`SELECTION` Research Records (question chosen, alternatives, value and
cost estimates with declared basis, horizon, stopping rule). It proposes;
it admits nothing. Its effectiveness is measurable under §III.4's existing
rule plus two derived measures: how often a chosen experiment's resolution
changed a decision, and the error of its estimates. It is replaced by an
Authorization act, `0/0/0`. Screening dispositions (§3.1 (d)) are part of
its record: a selection method that rejected the item that later became a
funded Theme under another lineage has a measurable miss.

Budgets: AIE never touches `ΔO/ΔS/ΔA`; it allocates research budget by
proposal within its Authorization; capital moves only by Allocation
Transition on a Strategy's own forward record.

### 5.3 Capacity outside preferred paths

The discovery scope (§3.1 (b)) carries the units a selection method does
not direct. The Principal sets the fraction. Use is visible as a derived
view over `SCREEN` and non-Theme Research Records.

### 5.4 Authority of research selection

Clear for the decision (Principal by Transition; policy under D-11). Clear
for the method once it is an Agent Design under an ordinary Research
Authorization. Undefined for the record until FR-7 is admitted.
